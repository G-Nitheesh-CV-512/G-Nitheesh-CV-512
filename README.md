I am a first-year PhD Research Scholar at **IIIT Allahabad**, developing my research direction in Computer Vision. I am interested in how visual models recognize unfamiliar categories, respond to changing environments, and recover useful information from images.

**Currently learning:** Deep Learning for Computer Vision.  
**Academic direction:** AI research, reproducible experimentation, and a long-term career spanning research and teaching.

## Research interests

These are areas I am exploring as an early-stage researcher.

### 01 &nbsp; Visual understanding
**Open-World Semantic Segmentation** · **Object Detection** · **Change Detection**  
Understanding scenes, recognizing objects, and identifying meaningful visual change.

### 02 &nbsp; Robust & adaptive vision
**Continual Test-Time Adaptation** · **Domain Adaptation** · **Image Restoration**  
Investigating how visual models can respond to distribution shifts and degraded imagery.

### 03 &nbsp; Modern vision models
**Vision Transformers** · **Foundation Models**  
Studying representations and learning methods for Deep Learning in Computer Vision and Image Processing.
